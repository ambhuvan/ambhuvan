# Hi, I'm Bhuvan Anand Manchiganahalli 👋

I am a Computer Science researcher (B.Tech CSE, PES University '26) working at the intersection of **resilient data infrastructure**, **physical-world sensing**, and **sociotechnical civic & healthcare tools**. I enjoy building end-to-end systems—from low-latency ingestion pipelines and edge sensors to accessible interfaces shaped by on-the-ground community fieldwork in India.

---

### 🔬 Current Research & Fieldwork Focus

* **Civic Data Intermediaries & Environmental Governance:** Built an automated Python/FastAPI & PostgreSQL pipeline ingesting real-time noise (dB) and air-quality telemetry across 10+ Karnataka State Pollution Control Board (KSPCB) stations in Bengaluru, translating regulatory threshold breaches into ready-to-file municipal complaint dossiers alongside local Resident Welfare Associations (RWAs) and DataMeet Bengaluru.
* **Context-Aware Health Sensing & Accessible Interfaces:** Investigated how wearable mobility pipelines strip domestic context in post-stroke home rehabilitation; partnered with neuro-physiotherapists in Bengaluru to prototype *Attendant Mode* (React/Figma + Python/HealthKit schemas) for 5-second regional-language caregiver voice annotations, alongside 3D LiDAR + camera trajectory tracking at MARS Labs.
* **Fault-Tolerant Storage & High-Velocity Ingestion:** Co-inventor on a filed Indian patent (*Application No. 202641*) for DAG-based integrity-tagged checkpoint recovery in zero-trust file pipelines, and first author of a Scopus-indexed paper (*WCMRI Singapore 2026*) on dual-buffered asynchronous microservices sustaining 200,000 concurrent requests with 0% packet loss.
* **Hardware Protocols & Inclusive Authentication:** Engineered Spring Boot & Web Bluetooth TLV packet-debugging tools for POS hardware at Evolute Group while analyzing and redesigning rigid TOTP authentication flows that locked out rural and elderly users.
